# PRODUCT.md

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Type

Wedding Organizer Landing Page

### Business Model

Service-based business.

The primary product is not a physical item, but a **professional wedding planning and coordination service** that helps couples plan, prepare, and execute their wedding celebration.

### Core Product

Wedding planning and event coordination for couples who want a professionally managed wedding experience.

### Primary Business Outcome

Generate qualified wedding inquiries and consultation requests.

### Primary Conversion

**Start Planning / Request Consultation**

---

# 2\. Product Definition

## What Is Being Sold?

The business sells more than wedding-day coordination.

The actual value proposition consists of:

```
Planning
+
Coordination
+
Vendor Management
+
Execution
+
Experience
+
Peace of Mind
```

The client is responsible for transforming the couple's vision into an organized and well-executed wedding experience.

---

# 3\. Customer Problem

Planning a wedding involves many moving parts:

- Vendors
- Venue
- Decoration
- Catering
- Photography
- Makeup
- Attire
- Ceremony
- Reception
- Timeline
- Guest coordination
- Family expectations
- Cultural traditions
- Wedding-day logistics

Without sufficient planning and coordination, couples may experience:

- Information overload
- Vendor coordination problems
- Timeline issues
- Miscommunication
- Last-minute decisions
- Unclear responsibilities
- Difficulty enjoying the wedding day itself

The product addresses this complexity by providing professional planning and coordination.

---

# 4\. Customer Need

The prospective customer needs:

### Practical Need

> Someone who can organize and coordinate the many details involved in a wedding.

### Emotional Need

> Someone they can trust so they can enjoy the wedding rather than constantly manage it.

### Social Need

> A wedding that reflects their relationship, family, culture, and desired celebration.

### Functional Outcome

A wedding that is properly planned and coordinated.

### Emotional Outcome

A couple who can be present and enjoy their wedding day.

---

# 5\. Target Customer

## Primary Audience

Couples who are preparing for their wedding and are considering professional wedding planning or coordination services.

### General Characteristics

- Currently planning or preparing for marriage
- Need help organizing wedding-related details
- Value professional coordination
- Want to reduce planning complexity
- Care about the quality of the wedding experience
- May require coordination across multiple vendors
- May want traditional, modern, intimate, or customized celebrations

---

# 6\. Audience Segments

The actual audience segments should be validated with the client.

Potential segments include:

### 01 — Full Wedding Planning

Couples who want professional assistance from early planning through execution.

Primary need:

> "Help us plan everything."

---

### 02 — Wedding Day Coordination

Couples who have planned much of the wedding themselves but need a professional team to coordinate the event.

Primary need:

> "We've prepared everything. We need someone to make sure everything runs smoothly."

---

### 03 — Traditional Wedding

Couples whose wedding includes cultural or traditional ceremonies.

Primary need:

> "We want our traditions to be handled properly while keeping the event organized."

---

### 04 — Intimate Wedding

Couples planning a smaller, more personal celebration.

Primary need:

> "We want something meaningful and personal without unnecessary complexity."

---

### 05 — Customized Wedding

Couples with a specific concept or vision that requires customized planning.

Primary need:

> "We already have a vision. We need someone who can bring it to life."

---

# 19\. Product Objections

The landing page should anticipate common objections.

### Objection 01

> "Do we really need a wedding organizer?"

Response strategy:

Demonstrate the complexity of planning and the value of professional coordination.

---

### Objection 02

> "Will they understand our vision?"

Response strategy:

Show real wedding work and explain the planning approach.

---

### Objection 03

> "Can they handle our type of wedding?"

Response strategy:

Use relevant portfolio examples and clearly explain capabilities.

---

### Objection 04

> "Can they handle traditional elements?"

Response strategy:

Show relevant real-world experience if confirmed.

---

### Objection 05

> "What exactly do they handle?"

Response strategy:

Provide clear service descriptions and process information.

---

### Objection 06

> "How much does it cost?"

Response strategy:

Present packages or provide a clear consultation/proposal pathway depending on the client's business model.

---

### Objection 07

> "What happens after I contact them?"

Response strategy:

Explain the consultation and planning process.
